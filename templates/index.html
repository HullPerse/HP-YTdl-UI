yt-dlp needs YouTube session cookies. Export cookies from your browser (visit YouTube while logged in, then export via extension or yt-dlp command).

If auto-detect reports "locked": close your browser completely (check Task Manager for lingering processes) and try again.

Manual Cookies

Export cookies from your browser and paste below:

  • Extension — Install "cookies.txt" (Chrome/Edge) or "cookies.txt" (Firefox), visit YouTube, click the extension icon, and export.
  • yt-dlp command — Close your browser, then run: yt-dlp --cookies-from-browser BROWSER --cookies cookies.txt and paste the file content.
  • Supported browsers: chrome, edge, firefox, brave, opera, vivaldi, chromium
